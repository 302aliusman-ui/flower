My love,

They say a bouquet speaks when words fall short. So let these roses say what I sometimes fumble: you are — and have been from the very first moment — the best thing in my life.

I love you on the loud days and the quiet ones. When you're radiant, and when you're unravelling. I love the person you are, and the person I get to become beside you.

These roses will fade. What I feel for you will not. If I could, I would hand you a bouquet like this every morning for the rest of our lives — just to watch you smile the way only you can.

I love you. Today, tomorrow, and every ordinary day in between.

— completely and always, yours
